# New Zealand — RN pathway (overview)

**Audience:** internationally qualified nurses exploring NZ registration.
**Not legal advice.** Confirm with Nursing Council of New Zealand and Immigration NZ.

## Regulator
- Nursing Council of New Zealand (NCNZ): https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/
- Internationally qualified nurse registration information is published by NCNZ — use their site for current application steps, fees, and competence requirements.

## Registration concepts
- Practising as a nurse in NZ requires registration / practising certificate processes under NCNZ rules.
- IQN pathways may include qualification assessment, English language evidence, and competence assessment where required.
- **Uncertain:** exact fee tables and processing SLAs — always take numbers from NCNZ pages on the day you apply.

## English language
- NCNZ sets English language requirements for registration. Check the current standard on the Council site rather than third-party summaries.

## Migration / work
- Immigration New Zealand: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/
- Visa options (skilled migrant, accredited employer work visa, etc.) change over time — verify on Immigration NZ.
- **Uncertain:** occupation lists, points, and wait times — do not invent.

## Quick checklist
1. Open NCNZ international registration pages.
2. Confirm English + competence requirements currently published.
3. Plan visa via Immigration NZ separately from registration.
