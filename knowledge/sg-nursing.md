# Singapore — RN pathway (overview)

**Audience:** internationally qualified nurses exploring Singapore registration.
**Not legal advice.** Confirm with Singapore Nursing Board and MOM.

## Regulator
- Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) via Singapore Medical Council / health professionals portal: https://www.healthprofessionals.gov.sg/snb
- SNB registers nurses and midwives and publishes application information for foreign-trained nurses.

## Registration concepts
- Practising nursing in Singapore requires registration / enrolment with SNB under current rules.
- Foreign-trained applicants typically need documented qualifications, verification, and may face additional assessments — **follow SNB's current checklist**.
- **Uncertain:** processing times and fee amounts — read SNB notices; do not copy blog numbers.

## Work passes
- Ministry of Manpower (MOM) governs work passes: https://www.mom.gov.sg/
- Employment Pass / S Pass eligibility depends on employer sponsorship and MOM criteria.
- **Uncertain:** salary thresholds and quota rules change — check MOM, never invent figures here.

## Quick checklist
1. Read SNB foreign-trained nurse application pages.
2. Confirm documents and any required tests with SNB.
3. Align employer / work pass planning with MOM guidance.
