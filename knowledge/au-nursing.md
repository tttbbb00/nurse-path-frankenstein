# Australia — RN pathway (overview)

**Audience:** internationally qualified nurses exploring Australian registration.
**Not legal advice.** Confirm with AHPRA / NMBA and Home Affairs.

## Regulators
- Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA): https://www.ahpra.gov.au/
- Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA): https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/
- NMBA registration standards and international applicants information live on the NMBA site — start at the registration / internationally qualified nurses sections.

## Registration concepts
- Nurses must hold registration with NMBA (via AHPRA processes) to practise.
- Pathways for internationally qualified nurses (IQNs) may involve qualification assessment, English language requirements, and/or an outcomes-based assessment — **requirements change**; read current NMBA pages.
- AHPRA registration homepage: https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration.aspx

## English language
- NMBA publishes English language skills registration standards. Acceptable tests and scores are defined by the Board, not by this app.
- Always open the current NMBA English language skills standard from: https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/

## Migration / work
- Skilled migration and visas are managed by the Australian Department of Home Affairs: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/
- Nursing may appear on skilled occupation lists depending on the program year — **lists change**. Check Home Affairs, not archived blogs.
- **Uncertain:** points scores, invitation rounds, processing times — mark as check official sources only.

## Quick checklist
1. Read NMBA internationally qualified nurse guidance.
2. Confirm English language standard currently in force.
3. Separate **registration** planning from **visa** planning (Home Affairs).
4. Do not invent salaries or wait times.
